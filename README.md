## Project Highlights:

- **Finance View:** Financial planning and expense management through analysis of Profit & Loss statements, net sales trends, and top-performing products and customers.
- **Sales View:** Driving sales revenue with insights into customer and product performance, including net sales, gross margin %, and unit economics.
- **Marketing View:** Data-driven strategies focusing on product, market, regional, and customer performance, tracking gross margin and net profit %.
- **Supply Chain View:** Metrics for efficient supply chain management such as forecast accuracy, net error, and product-level analysis.
- **Executive View:** Real-time executive dashboard showcasing revenue by division, customers, products, and channels.
